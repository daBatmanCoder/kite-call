Safety. Offshore = don’t go. Side-offshore is a walk of shame or worse. This app ranks Open-Meteo 10 m model wind for daylight hours (10:00–19:00 Europe/Amsterdam). It is not a KNMI warning, not a tide table, and not local knowledge. Check flags, zonering, oysters, current, and whether the spot is even open (Grevelingendam Noord is seasonal). Scores are honest, not hype.
